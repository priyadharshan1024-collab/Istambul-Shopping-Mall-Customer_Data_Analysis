# Istambul-Shopping-Mall-Customer_Data_Analysis
I analyzed customer shopping transactions from 10 malls in Istanbul between 2021 and 2023 to understand what was driving sales and customer spending. Using MySQL, Excel, Power Query and Power BI, I cleaned and analyzed the data across customer demographics, product categories, payment methods, mall performance and monthly revenue trends. The final Power BI dashboard turns these findings into a clear view of high-value customer groups, top-performing categories and locations, and changing sales patterns, helping identify where marketing, inventory and sales efforts could be focused to improve business performance.
